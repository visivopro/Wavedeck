Wavedeck

A classic-style music player for FLAC and MP3, with skins, an equalizer, visualizations and a modern media library.

Free software. Built with Electron, butterchurn (Milkdrop visualizations), music-metadata, JSZip and the Webamp Modern skin engine (MIT license, © Jordan Eldredge and contributors), which renders Winamp 5, Windows Media Player and Sonique skins.
Loads classic Winamp 2.x skins (.wsz). Winamp is a trademark of its respective owner; Wavedeck is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by it.
